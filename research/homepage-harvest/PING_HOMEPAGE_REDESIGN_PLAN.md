# PING HOMEPAGE REDESIGN PLAN

Date: 2026-09-17. Status: harvest synthesis, no implementation. Current homepage: 14 sections, 408-line page.tsx. Website repo untouched except the items already recorded.

## 1. CURRENT HOMEPAGE DIAGNOSIS

The homepage's problem is not insufficient content. It is too many ideas receiving similar visual weight. The current 14-section sequence:

1. Hero ("Systems that remember, agents you can trust") plus HeroSystem visual, CTAs "Explore the architecture" and "PING Social"
2. System strip
3. Continuity problem ("Most AI starts from zero every session")
4. Knowledge loop ("Record before intelligence")
5. TenantOS ("The operating model for agent-ready businesses")
6. Business graph ("PING understands relationships, not just documents")
7. Agent authority ("Why PING agents are different")
8. Evidence and replay ("Explainable after the fact")
9. Execution infrastructure ("Where PING is going")
10. PING Social applied layer ("The applied layer")
11. Build journal ("From the build")
12. Roadmap ("Where this is headed")
13. Newsletter ("Follow the build")
14. Contact CTA

The reference audit confirmed the outlier: Railway runs about 6 sections, Fly.io about 8, Modal about 8, Koyeb about 10, Deno about 10, Resend about 12 short sections under strict weight discipline. None of them runs 14 sections at equal weight. The fix is hierarchy: a hero, 3 to 5 signature moments, supporting sections, proof, close.

Three deeper problems:
- The applied layer sits at section 10 of 14. Perplexity's harvested pattern (the homepage IS the applied demo) and the concrete anchors the abstract. The applied beat must move much earlier in the visitor's attention, even if the full gallery stays later.
- Claims travel without adjacent receipts. The harvest's single most repeated trust device across all groups is claim-proof adjacency. PING's current sections make large claims (continuity, authority, replay) with no adjacent evidence specimen.
- Nothing on the page is labeled with its real maturity. Statuses across the whole plan remain PENDING VERIFICATION against the /home/nolan/ping repo audit. The redesign assumes a structural maturity-label system regardless of what the audit finds.

## 3. WHAT PING SHOULD BORROW CONCEPTUALLY

- Demonstrate first, explain second. The hero shows the system mid-work; explanation follows.
- A verb spine for the whole page: one loop, told as a story with a beginning, middle, and end.
- The closed loop in three or four verbs, last verb feeding the first, repeated everywhere.
- Authority as numbered layers with visible gates and at least one honest OFF.
- One authority, many runtimes: the convergence grammar, the three-beat (where execution happens, what PING provides, what that guarantees).
- Evidence as interaction: inline evidence markers to preview to full lineage.
- Claim-proof adjacency as a page-wide layout rule: no capability block without an adjacent receipt or honest status label.
- Maturity labels on every capability, statuses pending audit verification.
- A purpose clause on every section.
- Terminal-slash eyebrows (/observe /remember /connect /act /prove) for page voice.
- Monospace rationed to action, sequence, evidence.
- Type-weight discipline: moments versus mentions, enforced by the type system.
- Continuous-tense microcopy for operational aliveness, no fake numbers attached.
- Deliberate mobile compositions for signature visuals only; supporting sections reflow.

## 4. WHAT PING SHOULD NOT COPY

- Any site's branding, artwork, illustration style, or visual identity.
- Vercel's "where X" frame verbatim; Cloudflare's "Region: Earth" phrasing; Perplexity's reseller framing; Mercury's finance couplets; Raycast's emotional wording; LangChain's exact verbs; Karpathy's "autonomy slider" phrase on the page.

- Decorative animation. Motion depicts system activity or it does not ship.

The page earns 3 to 5 unforgettable moments. Recommended: five. Signature moments 1 through 3, 6, and 7.

**Moment 3: Intelligence is not authority.**

**Moment 4: One authority, many runtimes.**

## 8. MOTION SYSTEM

- Motion depicts system activity, never decoration. Ambient drift in the event plane, trace rendering in the evidence card, looping "system in motion" visuals per signature moment (Modal's pattern).
- The hero's verb rotation follows Vercel's accessibility pattern: reserved width, screen-reader sentence, reduced-motion static fallback.
- Looping visuals ship with poster frames and tap-to-play on mobile. No autoplay assumptions.
- Scroll-triggered reveals: keep them subtle and fast; the harvest could not recover exact easing or durations from any site, so these remain the implementation's craft judgment. Reduced motion disables all non-essential animation.
- No fake live data in motion: no fake tickers, no fabricated terminal output, no mock dashboards. Sample traces labeled as samples.

## 9. DIAGRAM SYSTEM

- One visual voice across all diagrams: minimal line schematics in PING's palette on dark (Deno's illustration-voice lesson: consistency itself is the brand). Zero exceptions.
- The convergence grammar (many to one through a governed plane) is the recurring structure for the runtime story. Progressive structural repetition (same frame, growing scale) for the growth story. Same-event-multiple-handlers for the proof.
- Every diagram carries a purpose clause and, where relevant, an honesty label: live, illustrative, or direction.
- No copied aesthetics: no Retool arcs, no Neo4j node-link styling, no Linear card styling, no ASCII gimmicks. PING's lines, PING's rhythm.

## 10. TYPOGRAPHY SYSTEM

- Three tiers with fixed jobs (Linear's discipline, PING's values): display weight for the 5 signature moments only; UI/emphasis weight for supporting sections; reading weight for body. Supporting sections are never promoted to moment weight. The type system enforces the editorial hierarchy.
- Luminance ramp for text tiers; PING's accent rationed to CTAs and live-status indicators. Decorative accent use is a defect.
- Every section gets a terminal-slash eyebrow (/observe, /remember, /connect, /act, /prove, /govern) in one canonical taxonomy, used relentlessly across sections, diagrams, and the journal.
- A purpose clause on every section heading ("Evidence (so every answer can show its work)").
- The standard supporting block: bold claim (3 to 6 words) plus one mechanism sentence with concrete nouns. Never a claim without its mechanism sentence. Lists get two reading speeds: bold verb plus one-line mechanism.
- Monospace in three rationed roles only: the one real invocation, sequence numbering, measured values. Everything else stays in display/body faces.
- Continuous-tense operational microcopy for aliveness ("events streaming in", "knowledge compounding"), never with fake numbers.

## 11. MOBILE STRATEGY

- Every signature visual gets a purpose-built mobile composition, budgeted as separate design work (Together AI and Koyeb are the only confirmed precedents; this is the rule, not the exception).
- Mobile transformations per moment: living hero becomes one evidence card with one timestamp; the loop becomes a vertical stepper or tabbed beats; the graph canvas becomes a tappable node list plus detail cards; the convergence diagram becomes a vertical sources-to-plane-to-outcomes flow; the authority toggles become a settings list; the trace becomes a condensed scrolling log; the autonomy gradient becomes stepped buttons.
- Simplify, do not shrink: decide per section what gets removed on mobile, not just resized. Display type steps down (72 to 48 to 32 discipline), section spacing drops, cards go multi-column to single column.
- No hover-dependent interactions anywhere. Every interaction designed tap-first.
- Exact current PING mobile behavior is UNKNOWN (not yet inspected); the implementation pass must design mobile compositions from the desktop moments, not from the current mobile page.
