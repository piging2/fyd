Preserving bits is not preserving meaning. Episode 1 climbs a seven-rung ladder (bits, file, format, meaning, context, authority, civilisation), breaks every rung on purpose, and converts what survives into a design spec: design for the carrier, not the receiver. Every claim on the show is graded live: receipt, BS, or thought experiment. The grades stick.

## The short version

A system that cannot remember cannot be trusted with anything that matters. That argument starts here, in this episode, and the rest of the series, and the rest of PING, is what happens when you refuse to let it go.
