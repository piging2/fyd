About Us

Hello, my name is Paul Brisco.

Electrical work has always been a passion of mine. My dedication to learning about the trade, started in 2000.  I had  many years of apprenticeship along side other dedicated professionals.

 In my 20 years as an electrician,  I have masters and journeyman’s license. I have taken many classes and have taught class at Mesa state college and local 969 union. I have taken classes with neca in Denver as well. 

My goal is to provide honest, affordable electrical work to everyone. From

Residential to Commercial and everything in between.
